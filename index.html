Definitions & Interpretation

Gross profit = sales revenue − cost of goods sold (COGS). Operating profit in this project is calculated as gross profit − expenses recorded in the EXPENSES file. It is a simplified operating result based on the supplied dataset, not an audited statutory profit figure.

Inventory value is taken from the PRODUCTS file. Inventory and vendor/purchase metrics are current dataset summaries and are not changed by the reporting-month filter.
