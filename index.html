Definitions & interpretation

Gross profit = sales revenue − cost of goods sold (COGS). Operating profit in this project is calculated as gross profit − expenses recorded in the EXPENSES file. It is a simplified operating result based on the supplied dataset, not audited statutory profit.

Inventory value is taken from the PRODUCTS file. Inventory and vendor/purchase metrics are current dataset summaries and are not changed by the sales-month filter. Monthly expense comparisons use the expense dates in the supplied file.
